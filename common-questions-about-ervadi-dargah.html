2. Where is Ervadi Dargah located?

Ervadi Dargah is located in Ervadi village, Kadaladi Taluk, Ramanathapuram district, Tamil Nadu, India. It is situated approximately 27 km from Ramanathapuram town. The nearest major railway station is Ramanathapuram Railway Station, and the nearest airport is Madurai Airport (approx. 150 km away). Visitors can easily reach the shrine via well-connected roads, buses, and taxis.

14. Ervadi Dargah history

Ervadi Dargah is dedicated to Hazrat Qutbus Sultan Syed Ibrahim Shaheed Badusha Nayagam (RA), who travelled from Arabia to spread Islamic teachings in South India. The shrine represents centuries of faith, communal harmony, and hosts the famous Santhanakoodu Festival.
