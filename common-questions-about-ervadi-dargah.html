Common Questions & Answers About Ervadi Dargah

Find comprehensive answers to frequently asked questions regarding Ervadi Dargah history, travel routes from major cities, accommodations, room options, timings, and pilgrim facilities.

1. Latest Ervadi Dargah Articles & Insights

Explore the history, heritage, traditions, and spiritual significance of Ervadi Dargah in Ramanathapuram District, Tamil Nadu. Stay informed about religious practices, the annual Sandanakoodu (Uroos) Festival, nearby dargahs, and travel guides designed for devotees, pilgrims, and researchers.

2. Where is Ervadi Dargah located?

Ervadi Dargah is located in Ervadi village, Kadaladi Taluk, Ramanathapuram district, Tamil Nadu, India. It is situated approximately 27 km from Ramanathapuram town. The nearest major railway station is Ramanathapuram Railway Station, and the nearest airport is Madurai Airport (approx. 150 km away). Visitors can easily reach the shrine via well-connected roads, buses, and taxis.

14. Ervadi Dargah history

Ervadi Dargah is dedicated to Hazrat Qutbus Sultan Syed Ibrahim Shaheed Badusha Nayagam (RA), who travelled from Arabia to spread Islamic teachings in South India. The shrine represents centuries of faith, communal harmony, and hosts the famous Santhanakoodu Festival.

15. Ervadi Dargah timings

The Dargah complex is generally open 365 days a year. The Holy Mazhar is typically open mornings after Fajr prayer and evenings from Asr until Isha prayer. Darbaar Guest House also offers complimentary guided tours to all local shrines.

16. Hotels near Kattupalli Dargah

Darbaar Guest House provides convenient access not only to the Main Dargah but also to Kattupalli Dargah, making it an optimal location for complete Ziyarat tours.

17. Best time to visit Ervadi

While visitors come year-round, the winter months from December to February offer the most pleasant weather. Many pilgrims also visit during the Islamic month of Dhu al-Qi'dah for the annual Santhanakoodu Festival.
