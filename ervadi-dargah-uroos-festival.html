Ervadi Santhanakoodu (Uroos) Festival

The annual Ervadi Santhanakoodu (Uroos) Festival is a month-long, multi-faith celebration of national integrity held at the holy Ervadi Dargah in Ramanathapuram district, Tamil Nadu. Commemorating the anniversary of Hazrat Sulthan Syed Ibrahim Shaheed Badhusha, the festival draws over 15 lakh devotees from across India and worldwide destinations including Sri Lanka, the UK, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, and Kuwait.

Key Events & Sacred Rituals

1

Ceremonial Flag Hoisting & Lowering

Marks the official beginning and grand conclusion of the month-long Uroos festival at the central mosque.

2

Grand Midnight Procession

A magnificent cultural procession featuring a decorated elephant, dancing horses, and traditional folk art troupes.

3

Santhanakoodu (Sandal Smearing)

The holy application of fragranced sandal paste (Santhanakoodu) onto the sacred rouza (tomb) of the saint.

4

Thabarruk Distribution (Nei Choru)

Following the rituals, traditional blessed Nei Choru (ghee rice) is served to thousands of visiting pilgrims.
