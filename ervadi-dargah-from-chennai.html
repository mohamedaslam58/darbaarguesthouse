Ervadi Dargah from Chennai - Complete Travel Guide

A Spiritual Journey to Ramanathapuram, Tamil Nadu

Are you planning to visit Ervadi Dargah from Chennai? Ervadi Dargah, located in Ramanathapuram district of Tamil Nadu, is one of the well-known spiritual pilgrimage destinations in South India. Devotees and visitors from Chennai and different parts of Tamil Nadu travel to Ervadi to visit the holy shrine of Hazrat Sulthan Syed Ibrahim Shaheed Badhusha Nayagam.

Whether you are travelling with your family, friends or as part of a group, planning your journey in advance can make your pilgrimage more comfortable and convenient.

Ervadi Dargah welcomes visitors throughout the year, with particularly large gatherings during the annual Santhanakoodu festival.

Distance from Chennai to Ervadi Dargah

The approximate road distance from Chennai to Ervadi is around 510 to 515 kilometres, depending on your starting point in Chennai and selected highway route. Travelling by car or direct bus takes approximately 8 to 10 hours depending on traffic, road conditions, and rest stops.

Ramanathapuram is the nearest major railway destination for visitors travelling by train from Chennai. From Ramanathapuram railway station, you can easily hire a taxi or use local bus transport to reach Ervadi Dargah (approx. 27 km).

How to Reach Ervadi Dargah from Chennai

Choose the best mode of transport suited for your travel group and budget

1. Chennai to Ervadi Dargah by Train

Travelling by train is a convenient and relaxed option for families and elderly pilgrims:

  • Start your journey from Chennai Egmore (MS).
  • Book an express or overnight train towards Ramanathapuram Railway Station (RMD).
  • After reaching Ramanathapuram, hire a taxi or take a local bus to Ervadi Dargah.
  • The railway station is approximately 27 km from the Dargah.

* Check latest train timings and ticket availability through IRCTC prior to travelling.

2. Chennai to Ervadi Dargah by Bus

Multiple government (SETC) and private sleeper/AC bus services operate daily on this route:

  • Board an overnight bus from Koyambedu (CMBT) or Kilambakkam (KCBT) in Chennai towards Ramanathapuram.
  • Alight at Ramanathapuram Bus Stand or direct drop points near Ervadi turn-off.
  • Take a local connector bus or taxi directly to Ervadi Dargah.

During the annual Santhanakoodu festival, special government bus services are arranged from Chennai and other major Tamil Nadu cities.

3. Chennai to Ervadi Dargah by Car

Travelling by private car or cab is ideal for families seeking maximum flexibility with timing and food breaks.

  • Route: Chennai → Chengalpattu → Villupuram → Trichy → Pudukkottai → Ramanathapuram → Ervadi.
  • Approx. Distance: 510–515 km.
  • Estimated Driving Time: 8 to 10 hours.

4. Chennai to Ervadi Dargah by Flight

Visitors who prefer air travel can opt for short domestic flights from Chennai Airport (MAA):

  • Nearest Airport: Madurai Airport (IXM) ~ 115 km from Ervadi, or Tuticorin Airport (TCR) ~ 125 km.
  • Fly from Chennai to Madurai/Tuticorin.
  • Hire a prepaid taxi or take an intercity bus from the airport to Ervadi Dargah.
