Ervadi Dargah from Bangalore - Complete Travel Guide

Distance, Travel Routes, Transport Options & Accommodation for Pilgrims

Are you planning a spiritual journey to Ervadi Dargah from Bangalore? Ervadi Dargah, located in Ervadi, Ramanathapuram district, Tamil Nadu, is one of the most well-known Islamic pilgrimage destinations in South India. Devotees from Bangalore and different parts of Karnataka visit this sacred shrine to offer prayers and pay their respects to Hazrat Qutbus Sultan Syed Ibrahim Shaheed Badusha Nayagam.

Whether you are travelling with your family, friends or as a group of devotees, this guide will help you understand how to reach Ervadi Dargah from Bangalore by road, train or flight.

Distance from Bangalore to Ervadi Dargah

The approximate road distance from Bangalore to Ervadi Dargah is around 500–560 kilometres, depending on the selected route. Travelling by car may take approximately 8–10 hours, depending on traffic, road conditions and stops.

Travel Details:

  • Starting Point: Bangalore, Karnataka
  • Destination: Ervadi Dargah, Ramanathapuram, Tamil Nadu
  • Approximate Road Distance: 500–560 km
  • Estimated Driving Time: 8–10 hours
  • Nearest Major Railway Station: Ramanathapuram (RMD)
  • Nearest Major Airport: Madurai Airport (IXM)

How to Reach Ervadi Dargah from Bangalore

There are several convenient ways to travel from Bangalore to Ervadi Dargah

1. By Road – Bangalore to Ervadi Dargah

Travelling by car or private vehicle is a convenient option for families and groups. You can travel from Bangalore towards Salem, Madurai, Ramanathapuram and finally Ervadi.

You can also consider private taxi services or intercity buses travelling towards Ramanathapuram.

2. By Train – Bangalore to Ervadi Dargah

Train travel is another convenient option for devotees travelling from Bangalore. You can check trains from Bangalore to Ramanathapuram Railway Station.

From Ramanathapuram, Ervadi Dargah can be reached by local bus, taxi or private vehicle (approx. 25–30 km away). Check the latest train schedules and availability before planning your journey.

3. By Flight – Bangalore to Ervadi Dargah

For visitors who prefer air travel, flights are available from Kempegowda International Airport, Bangalore, to Madurai Airport, subject to airline schedules.

After reaching Madurai, you can hire a taxi or arrange road transportation to Ervadi Dargah (approx. 130–140 km from Madurai, depending on the route).

Frequently Asked Questions – Bangalore to Ervadi Dargah

1. How far is Ervadi Dargah from Bangalore?

Ervadi Dargah is approximately 500–560 km from Bangalore by road. The journey generally takes around 8–10 hours by car, depending on the route and traffic.

2. How can I reach Ervadi Dargah from Bangalore?

You can reach Ervadi Dargah from Bangalore by private car, bus, train or flight. Ramanathapuram is the nearest major railway station, while Madurai is a convenient airport option.

3. Which is the nearest railway station to Ervadi Dargah?

Ramanathapuram Railway Station is the nearest major railway station, approximately 25–30 km from Ervadi Dargah.
