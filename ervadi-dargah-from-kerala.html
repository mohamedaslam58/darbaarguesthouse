Ervadi Dargah from Kerala - A Spiritual Journey to Tamil Nadu

Travel Guide & Accommodation Options for Devotees Travelling from Kerala

Ervadi Dargah, located in Ervadi, Ramanathapuram District, Tamil Nadu, is one of the well-known Islamic pilgrimage destinations in South India. Devotees from different parts of Kerala regularly travel to Ervadi to visit the holy shrine of Hazrat Sultan Syed Ibrahim Shaheed Badusha Nayagam (Radiyallahu Anhu).

The Dargah holds a special place among Muslim pilgrims, particularly those following the spiritual traditions of Kerala. Many families, devotees and visitors travel from Kerala to Ervadi to offer prayers, pay their respects and participate in religious occasions.

Whether you are planning a family pilgrimage, a short spiritual journey or a visit during the annual Santhanakoodu festival, Ervadi welcomes visitors throughout the year.

Kerala to Ervadi Dargah - A Pilgrimage for Devotees

Ervadi Dargah attracts devotees from various districts of Kerala, including Malappuram, Kozhikode, Kannur, Kasaragod, Thrissur, Palakkad, Ernakulam and Thiruvananthapuram.

Kerala has a rich tradition of visiting Sufi shrines, and Ervadi is among the important pilgrimage destinations outside the state. Devotees travel with their families and fellow pilgrims to seek blessings, offer prayers and experience the spiritual atmosphere of Ervadi Dargah.

The journey from Kerala to Ervadi can be planned by private car, bus or train, depending on your starting location and travel preferences.

Popular Kerala Cities from Where Devotees Visit Ervadi Dargah

Devotees planning a visit to Ervadi Dargah travel from several major cities and districts across Kerala:

  • Malappuram: Pilgrims from Malappuram and surrounding towns.
  • Kozhikode (Calicut): Visitors from Kozhikode and nearby areas.
  • Kannur: Devotees travelling from Kannur and surrounding regions.
  • Kasaragod: Pilgrims from the northern Kerala region.
  • Thrissur: Families and religious groups travelling from Thrissur.
  • Palakkad: Visitors from Palakkad and nearby towns.
  • Ernakulam (Kochi): Families and pilgrims from Kochi and surrounding locations.
  • Thiruvananthapuram: Devotees travelling from southern Kerala.
  • Kottayam: Pilgrims from Kottayam and nearby districts.
  • Alappuzha: Visitors from Alappuzha and surrounding areas.

* These are potential visitor-origin locations, not a verified ranking of visitor numbers.

How to Reach Ervadi Dargah from Kerala

1. By Road

Travelling by private car, taxi or bus is a convenient option for families and groups. Route options connect Kerala smoothly with Ramanathapuram District in Tamil Nadu.

2. By Train

Visitors can travel by train to Ramanathapuram Railway Station and arrange local transportation to Ervadi Dargah.

3. By Bus

Pilgrims can use available inter-state bus services towards Ramanathapuram and nearby towns, followed by local transport to Ervadi.

Travel schedules and connections may change, so check latest availability before starting your journey.

Visit During Santhanakoodu Festival

The annual Ervadi Santhanakoodu festival is an important religious occasion that attracts large numbers of devotees from Tamil Nadu, Kerala and other places.

The festival commemorates Hazrat Sultan Syed Ibrahim Shaheed Badusha Nayagam (Radiyallahu Anhu). Devotees gather to participate in religious ceremonies, special prayers and the traditional Santhanakoodu procession.

Important Note: Visitors travelling from Kerala during the festival season are strongly advised to plan their transportation and accommodation well in advance due to the large gathering of pilgrims.
