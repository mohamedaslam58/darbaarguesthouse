Visit Ervadi Dargah from Andhra Pradesh

Complete Pilgrimage Guide & Accommodation Information for Devotees from AP

Ervadi Dargah, located in Ramanathapuram district of Tamil Nadu, is an important spiritual destination visited by devotees and pilgrims from different parts of India. Known for its religious significance and peaceful atmosphere, Ervadi Dargah attracts visitors seeking blessings, spiritual comfort and an opportunity to offer prayers.

Every year, devotees from Andhra Pradesh travel to Ervadi Dargah with their families and fellow pilgrims. Whether you are planning a family pilgrimage, a group visit or an individual spiritual journey, Ervadi offers a welcoming destination for your religious visit.

If you are travelling from Andhra Pradesh, this guide will help you understand the available travel options, nearby places and accommodation facilities for a comfortable journey.

How to Reach Ervadi Dargah from Andhra Pradesh

Visitors from AP can reach Ervadi Dargah through trains, buses, or private vehicles depending on their departure city

1. Travel by Train

Travelling by train is one of the convenient options for pilgrims coming from major cities in Andhra Pradesh.

You can check available train connections towards Ramanathapuram or nearby railway stations in Tamil Nadu. From Ramanathapuram, you can hire a taxi or use local transportation to reach Ervadi Dargah.

2. Travel by Bus

Pilgrims can explore inter-state bus connections from major Andhra Pradesh cities towards Tamil Nadu.

Depending on the available services, travellers may need to change buses at major transport hubs before continuing to Ramanathapuram or Ervadi.

3. Travel by Private Car

Families and groups travelling together can consider a private car or rented vehicle. This option offers flexibility to plan stops, travel at a comfortable pace and visit nearby religious destinations.

Frequently Asked Questions - Ervadi Dargah from Andhra Pradesh

1. Where is Ervadi Dargah located?

Ervadi Dargah is located in Ervadi village, Ramanathapuram district, Tamil Nadu, India.

2. How can I reach Ervadi Dargah from Andhra Pradesh?

You can travel by train, bus or private vehicle. Depending on your departure city, you can plan a journey towards Ramanathapuram and continue to Ervadi by local transport.

3. Which railway station is nearest to Ervadi Dargah?

Ramanathapuram Railway Station is one of the nearest railway stations, approximately 25–30 km from Ervadi.

4. Can families from Andhra Pradesh visit Ervadi Dargah?

Yes. Families, individuals and groups from Andhra Pradesh visit Ervadi Dargah throughout the year.

5. Is accommodation available near Ervadi Dargah?

6. Can I book family rooms in Ervadi?

Yes. You can contact Darbaar Guest House to enquire about family rooms, suite accommodation and availability for your travel dates.

7. When is the Ervadi Santhanakoodu Festival celebrated?

The festival is celebrated annually during the Islamic month of Dhu al-Qi'dah. Visitors should check the officially announced dates before planning their journey.
