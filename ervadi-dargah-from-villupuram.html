Ervadi Dargah from Villupuram - Complete Travel Guide

Distance, Travel Routes, Transport Options & Accommodation for Pilgrims

Are you planning a spiritual journey to Ervadi Dargah from Villupuram, Tamil Nadu? Ervadi Dargah, located in Ervadi village in Ramanathapuram district, is one of the well-known spiritual pilgrimage destinations in Tamil Nadu. Devotees and visitors from Villupuram and neighbouring districts travel here throughout the year to visit the sacred shrine of Hazrat Qutbus Sultan Syed Ibrahim Shaheed Badhusha Nayagam.

Whether you are planning a family pilgrimage, a visit with friends, or a journey during the annual Santhanakoodu festival, this travel guide will help you plan your trip from Villupuram to Ervadi Dargah.

Distance from Villupuram to Ervadi Dargah

The approximate road distance from Villupuram to Ervadi Dargah is around 360–365 kilometres. Depending on the route, traffic conditions and travel arrangements, the journey by car may take approximately 5–6 hours.

Visitors can choose between bus, train and private car travel depending on their convenience and budget.

How to Reach Ervadi Dargah from Villupuram

Explore travel options by bus, train, or car from Villupuram

1. By Bus

Travelling by bus is a convenient option for visitors coming from Villupuram. You can look for buses operating from Villupuram towards Ramanathapuram.

From Ramanathapuram, local government buses and private vehicles are available to reach Ervadi Dargah. It is advisable to check the latest bus schedules and availability before starting your journey, particularly during festival seasons.

2. By Train

Visitors can travel from Villupuram Junction (VM) to Ramanathapuram Railway Station (RMD) by train. Depending on available train services, you may choose a direct connection or a connecting route.

After reaching Ramanathapuram Railway Station, you can hire a taxi or take a local bus to Ervadi Dargah.

3. By Car

Travelling by private car or taxi provides flexibility and convenience, especially for families and groups. The road journey from Villupuram to Ervadi takes approximately 5–6 hours, depending on traffic and road conditions.

Best Time to Visit Ervadi Dargah from Villupuram

Ervadi Dargah welcomes visitors throughout the year. You can plan your visit according to your family's convenience and travel schedule.

The annual Santhanakoodu festival is one of the most important occasions at Ervadi Dargah, attracting thousands of devotees from different cities and states. If you are planning to visit during the festival, consider arranging your transportation and accommodation in advance.
