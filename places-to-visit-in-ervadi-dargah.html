Explore Ramanathapuram & Ervadi

Ervadi is a historic coastal town in Tamil Nadu known for its spiritual heritage, eco-tourism spots, serene beaches, and rich cultural landmarks.

4.6 (1.4K reviews)

Ervadi Dargah Main Shrine

An 800-year-old tomb and shrine of Hazrat Sultan Syed Ibrahim Shaheed Badusha, an 18th-generation descendant of Prophet Muhammad. Draws pilgrims from across India, especially during December Sandanakoodu.
