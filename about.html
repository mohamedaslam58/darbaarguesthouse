About Us

Our goal is simple: to make your pilgrimage to Ervadi as comfortable and spiritually enriching as possible. By providing continuous maintenance, excellent facilities, and thoughtful guest services, we aim to be your true home away from home.

Our Mission

At Darbaar Guest House, our mission is to provide every pilgrim, family, and traveler visiting Ervadi Dargah with a peaceful, hygienic, and spiritually enriching sanctuary. We are dedicated to delivering genuine hospitality rooted in local culture, ensuring our guests enjoy the perfect balance of residential comfort, safety, and modern living amenities throughout their pilgrimage.


We strive to go beyond standard accommodation by offering comprehensive guest care—from specialized in-house cooking facilities and cookware support for family gatherings to dedicated tour guidance and prayer session coordination. Our goal is to make every visitor's journey seamless, worry-free, and deeply memorable.

WHY GUEST CHOOSE DARBAAR GUEST HOUSE?

Guests consistently choose Darbaar Guest House for our unyielding commitment to hospitality, cleanliness, and prime location near the sacred Ervadi Dargah. Designed specifically to cater to families and pilgrim groups, our property features well-appointed AC and Non-AC rooms equipped with 24/7 hot/cold water, high-speed Wi-Fi, and round-the-clock guest support."
