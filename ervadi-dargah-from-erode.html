Ervadi Dargah from Erode - Travel Guide, Distance, Bus & Train Information

Complete Pilgrimage Guide & Accommodation Information for Visitors from Erode

Are you planning to visit Ervadi Dargah from Erode, Tamil Nadu? Ervadi Dargah, located in Ramanathapuram district, is one of the well-known spiritual pilgrimage destinations in Tamil Nadu. Thousands of devotees from different parts of Tamil Nadu, Kerala and other states visit this sacred destination throughout the year to offer prayers and pay their respects to Hazrat Sultan Syed Ibrahim Shaheed Badhusha Oliyullah.

Whether you are travelling with your family, friends or as part of a group, this travel guide will help you understand how to reach Ervadi Dargah from Erode by road, bus and train.

How Far Is Ervadi Dargah from Erode?

Ervadi Dargah is approximately 340–350 km from Erode by road, depending on the route selected. The journey generally takes around 5–7 hours by car, depending on traffic, road conditions and stops along the way.

Visitors can plan their journey by private car, bus or train with onward road transportation to Ervadi.

How to Reach Ervadi Dargah from Erode?

Explore travel options by car, bus, or train from Erode

1. By Car – Erode to Ervadi Dargah

Travelling by private car or taxi is a convenient option for families and groups visiting Ervadi Dargah from Erode.

You can plan your journey through major towns and cities towards Madurai and Ramanathapuram before continuing to Ervadi. Check your preferred route using Google Maps before departure.

2. By Bus – Erode to Ervadi Dargah

Visitors can travel from Erode towards Ramanathapuram by available intercity bus services, with onward local transportation to Ervadi Dargah.

During the annual Ervadi Santhanakoodu Festival, special bus services may operate from different parts of Tamil Nadu to accommodate the increasing number of pilgrims. Check the latest TNSTC and private bus schedules before travelling.

3. By Train – Erode to Ervadi Dargah

Erode Junction (ED) is an important railway station with connections to several major cities in Tamil Nadu.

Visitors can check available train services from Erode to Ramanathapuram Railway Station (RMD). Depending on train schedules, a change of train may be required. From Ramanathapuram, Ervadi Dargah is approximately 25–30 km away by taxi, auto, or local bus.

Best Time to Visit Ervadi Dargah from Erode

Ervadi Dargah welcomes visitors throughout the year. You can plan your visit according to your family's travel schedule and religious preferences.

The annual Santhanakoodu Festival attracts a particularly large number of devotees. If you are planning to visit during the festival, it is advisable to arrange your transportation and accommodation in advance.
