Ervadi Dargah from Kambam - Travel Guide, Distance & Route

Complete Pilgrimage Guide & Accommodation Information for Visitors from Kambam (Cumbum)

Are you planning to visit Ervadi Dargah from Kambam (Cumbum), Theni District? Ervadi Dargah, located in Ramanathapuram District of Tamil Nadu, is an important spiritual destination visited by devotees from different parts of Tamil Nadu, Kerala and other states.

Whether you are travelling with family, friends or as part of a pilgrimage group, this guide will help you plan your journey from Kambam to Ervadi Dargah, understand the available transportation options and find suitable accommodation for your stay.

How to Reach Ervadi Dargah from Kambam

Visitors travelling from Kambam can reach Ervadi Dargah by road using private cars, taxis and public transportation. You can plan your journey through major towns and cities in Tamil Nadu towards Ramanathapuram District.

Depending on your selected route and traffic conditions, travel time and distance may vary. It is advisable to check live navigation directions before starting your journey.

Approximate Travel Information:

  • Starting Point: Kambam (Cumbum), Theni District
  • Destination: Ervadi Dargah, Ramanathapuram District
  • Approximate Road Distance: 225–250 km, depending on route
  • Estimated Car Journey: Around 4–5 hours

Commonly Listed Road Route:
Kambam → Theni → Usilampatti → Madurai → Paramakudi → Ramanathapuram → Ervadi Dargah.

* Travel distances and timings are estimates and should be checked using live navigation.

Transportation Options from Kambam

Explore options by car, bus, or train from Kambam

By Car or Taxi

Travelling by private car or taxi is a convenient option for families and groups. It allows you to travel comfortably and make stops along the way according to your preferences.

By Bus

Visitors can explore TNSTC bus services from Kambam towards Theni, Madurai and Ramanathapuram, followed by local transportation to Ervadi. Check the latest bus schedules before departure.

By Train

Visitors can consider travelling from Bodinayakkanur to Madurai and onward to Ramanathapuram by train. From Ramanathapuram, local transport or taxis can be used to reach Ervadi.
