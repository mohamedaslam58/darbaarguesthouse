How Can Visitors Book Accommodation?

Step-by-Step Guide to Booking Your Stay at Darbaar Guest House

Planning a spiritual visit to Ervadi Dargah? Finding comfortable and affordable accommodation is an important part of your journey. Whether you are visiting with family, travelling with friends or coming for religious purposes, Darbaar Guest House offers convenient daily rental accommodation to make your stay comfortable.

Visitors can easily enquire about room availability, facilities and rental prices before arriving in Ervadi.

Easy Booking Process

Follow these simple steps to reserve your room prior to your visit

1. Contact Darbaar Guest House

The easiest way to book your accommodation is to contact Darbaar Guest House directly by phone or WhatsApp. Our team can help you with room availability, room categories, rental charges and booking information.

2. Choose Your Preferred Room

Depending on your family size, budget and requirements, you can enquire about different accommodation options:

  • Family Rooms: Comfortable accommodation for families and groups.
  • Deluxe Rooms: Convenient rooms for visitors and pilgrims.
  • Single Rooms: Affordable accommodation for individual travellers.
  • AC and Non-AC Rooms: Options to suit different budgets.

3. Confirm Your Booking Details

Before confirming your stay, please share the following details with our team:

  • Guest name and contact number
  • Check-in and check-out dates
  • Number of adults and children
  • Preferred room type
  • Number of rooms required

Our team will check availability and share the applicable room tariff and booking confirmation details.

4. Plan Your Visit to Ervadi Dargah

Once your accommodation is confirmed, you can plan your visit to Ervadi Main Dargah and nearby Kattupalli Dargah.

Darbaar Guest House provides convenient accommodation for devotees, pilgrims and travellers visiting these important religious destinations.

5. Enjoy a Comfortable Stay

At Darbaar Guest House, we aim to make every guest's stay convenient and comfortable. Our facilities include:

  • Clean and well-maintained rooms
  • Cooking facilities for guests
  • Car parking facilities
  • 24/7 in-house assistance
  • Generator power backup
  • Accommodation for families and individual visitors

Why Choose Darbaar Guest House?

With around 40 years of hospitality service, Darbaar Guest House welcomes devotees, pilgrims and travellers visiting Ervadi Dargah. We understand the accommodation needs of families and visitors travelling from different parts of Tamil Nadu, Kerala and other states.

Our aim is to provide affordable accommodation, convenient facilities and helpful assistance throughout your stay.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. How can I book a room at Darbaar Guest House?

You can contact Darbaar Guest House directly by phone or WhatsApp to enquire about room availability, prices and booking confirmation.

2. Can I book accommodation before arriving in Ervadi?

Yes. Visitors are encouraged to contact us in advance to check room availability and arrange their accommodation before travelling.

3. Does Darbaar Guest House provide family rooms?

Yes. Family accommodation is available for visitors travelling with children and other family members, subject to availability.

4. Are cooking facilities available?

Yes. Selected accommodation options provide cooking facilities for guests who prefer to prepare their own meals.

5. Is parking available at Darbaar Guest House?

Yes. Parking facilities are available for guests travelling by car, subject to availability.

6. Can pilgrims book rooms for daily rent?

Yes. Daily rental accommodation is available for pilgrims, devotees and visitors planning short stays in Ervadi Dargah.

7. How can I check room prices?

Contact our team directly by phone or WhatsApp to receive the latest room tariffs based on your preferred dates and room category.
