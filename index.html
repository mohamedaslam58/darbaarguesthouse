About Us

Darbaar Guest House is a comfortable stay located near the famous Ervadi Dargah in Tamil Nadu. It offers rooms ranging from affordable to luxury options, ideal for pilgrims and travelers visiting the sacred shrine. With its convenient location and peaceful atmosphere, the guest house ensures a pleasant stay. It also provides a spacious cooking area for personal use and for preparing food for charity and donations.
