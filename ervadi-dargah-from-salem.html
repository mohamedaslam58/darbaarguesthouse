Ervadi Dargah from Salem - Complete Travel Guide

Distance, Routes, Transportation & Accommodation Guide for Devotees from Salem

Are you planning to visit Ervadi Dargah from Salem, Tamil Nadu? Ervadi Dargah, located in Ervadi, Ramanathapuram district, is one of the well-known Islamic pilgrimage destinations in South India. Devotees from Salem and other parts of Tamil Nadu visit this holy shrine to offer prayers and pay their respects to Hazrat Sulthan Syed Ibrahim Shaheed Badusha Nayagam.

Whether you are travelling with your family, friends, or as part of a pilgrimage group, this guide will help you plan your journey from Salem to Ervadi Dargah, including travel routes, transportation, accommodation and useful visitor information.

Salem to Ervadi Dargah Distance

The approximate road distance from Salem to Ervadi Dargah is around 320–330 kilometres, depending on the selected route. Travelling by car may take approximately 5–6 hours, depending on traffic and road conditions.

Ervadi is situated in Ramanathapuram district, Tamil Nadu, and is accessible by road from major cities across the state.

How to Reach Ervadi Dargah from Salem

Explore travel choices by car, train, or bus from Salem

1. By Car or Private Taxi

Travelling by car is a convenient option for families and groups visiting Ervadi Dargah from Salem.

Suggested Route:
Salem → Dindigul → Madurai → Ramanathapuram → Ervadi Dargah

You can travel with your family at your own pace and take breaks along the journey. Private taxi services are also available for a more comfortable pilgrimage.

2. By Train

Visitors can travel from Salem Junction (SA) to Ramanathapuram Railway Station (RMD) by train, depending on available train schedules.

After reaching Ramanathapuram, you can hire a private taxi or use local transportation to reach Ervadi Dargah. Check the latest train schedules and availability before planning your journey.

3. By Bus

You can travel from Salem to Madurai by bus and continue your journey towards Ramanathapuram.

From Ramanathapuram, take a local bus or taxi to Ervadi. This is another suitable option for devotees looking for affordable transportation.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How far is Ervadi Dargah from Salem?

Ervadi Dargah is approximately 320–330 km from Salem by road. The journey may take around 5–6 hours by car, depending on traffic and the selected route.

2. How can I travel from Salem to Ervadi Dargah?

You can travel by private car, taxi, train or bus. Train and bus journeys may require onward transportation from Ramanathapuram or Madurai.

3. Is there a direct train from Salem to Ervadi Dargah?

Ervadi does not have a railway station at the Dargah. You can check trains from Salem Junction to Ramanathapuram Railway Station and arrange local transportation to Ervadi.

4. Are accommodation facilities available near Ervadi Dargah?

Yes. Several guest houses and lodges provide accommodation for pilgrims and families visiting Ervadi Dargah. Darbaar Guest House offers family rooms, cooking facilities and other amenities.

5. Can families visit Ervadi Dargah?

Yes. Families, individuals and pilgrimage groups visit Ervadi Dargah throughout the year.

6. When is the best time to visit Ervadi Dargah?

Devotees visit throughout the year. Many visitors also plan their journey during the annual Ervadi Santhanakoodu and Uroos festival.
