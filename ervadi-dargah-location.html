Where is Ervadi Dargah Located?

Address, Location & Complete Travel Guide

Ervadi Dargah is located in Ervadi village, in the Ramanathapuram district of Tamil Nadu, India. It is a famous Islamic pilgrimage destination dedicated to the Sufi saint Sultan Syed Ibrahim Shaheed Badusha. The Dargah attracts thousands of devotees and visitors from different parts of Tamil Nadu, Kerala, and other states across India.

Ervadi Dargah is situated approximately 27 km from Ramanathapuram town and is accessible by road. It is a well-known spiritual destination for devotees seeking blessings, offering prayers, and visiting the sacred shrine.

Ervadi Dargah Location and Address

  • Dargah Name: Ervadi Dargah (Hazrat Sultan Syed Ibrahim Shaheed Badusha Dargah)
  • Village: Ervadi
  • District: Ramanathapuram
  • State: Tamil Nadu
  • Country: India
  • Nearest Major Town: Ramanathapuram (~27 km)
  • Nearest Railway Station: Ramanathapuram Railway Station
  • Nearest Airport: Madurai Airport (~150 km)

How to Reach Ervadi Dargah?

Visitors can reach Ervadi Dargah through different modes of transportation.

By Road

Ervadi is well connected by road from Ramanathapuram and nearby towns. Regular buses, taxis, and private vehicles are available to reach the Dargah.

By Train

Ramanathapuram Railway Station is the nearest major railway station. From there, visitors can hire a taxi or take a local bus to Ervadi Dargah.

By Air

Madurai Airport is the nearest major airport, approximately 150 km away by road. Visitors can travel from the airport to Ervadi by taxi or other available transport.
